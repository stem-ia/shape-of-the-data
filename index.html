Shape of the Data

I built these two pages for the Grade 8 math team's statistics and probability project. Students explore how histograms change with bin width and sample size, then graph real data sets on their own. Each page is a single file. It makes no network requests, sets no cookies and needs no login.

Shape of the Data Lab

The histogram lab: a guided pizza exploration, hockey, Pokemon and random samples.

 Shape of the Data Bank

Five raw data sets for students to graph themselves.
Built by Ben Wood, Digital Learning Coordinator, STEM Innovation Academy, Calgary. Data sources are listed at the bottom of each page.
